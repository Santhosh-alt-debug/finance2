Stackly Finance started in 2019 with one idea: people should understand every rupee they borrow, insure or invest.

Our story

We began as a small team frustrated by loan offers that hid fees in fine print. We built a lender that shows the full cost first, and the response told us we were not alone.

Today we serve more than 25,000 customers across Tamil Nadu and beyond, with loans, insurance and investments that follow the same rule: clear terms, no surprises.
